An explanation of the travel-planning data stored in this browser and the external services used when you choose to request research.

Beta information, not finalized legal terms. Operator identity, a support contact, region-specific privacy rights, retention practices for server/provider requests and legal notices require verification and approval before commercial launch.

01 / ON THIS DEVICE

What is saved locally

Wayfarer stores saved trip preferences and day-by-day itinerary drafts under wf-trips-v2, and arrival checklist progress under wf-checks-v2. This information stays in the current browser profile unless you export or share it yourself. It is not encrypted, account-synced or confidential from other scripts on the same website. Other people sharing this browser profile may be able to see it.

A local itinerary is a planning draft, not evidence of a booking, confirmed transport, live price or entry permission. Avoid entering passport numbers, payment details, private identity numbers or medical information into the planner.

02 / WHEN YOU REQUEST RESEARCH

Information that leaves this browser

When you request worldwide destinations, hotel/airport references, an AI journey or supplier travel research, your destination, related geographic coordinates or entered trip criteria may be sent to Wayfarer's server and then to relevant data providers. AI and paid supplier features are separately credential-gated and may be unavailable. Results can be incomplete, cached or outdated; they are not verified inventory.

Opening the map or photographic Earth can contact OpenStreetMap, NASA or a public image mirror. Following links to airlines, travel sites, Wikipedia, Google or other providers opens their websites, which have their own privacy practices. We do not represent third-party services as operated by Wayfarer.

04 / IMPORTANT LIMITS

Before you travel

Always confirm passports, visas, entry rules, official advisories, opening hours, airline schedules and final hotel prices directly with authoritative providers. A successful Wayfarer research result does not create a ticket or hotel reservation.

Formal privacy/terms, verified operator contact information and any rights-request process are outstanding launch requirements. This page is a transparency feature for the development beta, not a claim that those requirements are complete.
